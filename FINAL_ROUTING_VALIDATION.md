## 1. Routing Architecture

The navigation engine utilizes a unified polar pathfinding architecture:
- **Canonical Engine:** PolarRoutingEngine (`backend/src/optimization/polar_routing_engine.py`).
- **Coordinate Reference System:**
  - **Pathfinding & Metric Calculations:** Antarctic Polar Stereographic (`EPSG:3031`), unit meters, standard parallel $-71^\circ\text{S}$.
  - **Internal Spatial Storage:** `[x, y]` Cartesian meters.
  - **External Geographic API:** WGS84 `[latitude, longitude]` decimal degrees.
  - **Standard GeoJSON Payload:** `[longitude, latitude]` for Deck.gl and MapLibre layers.
- **Algorithm:** Grid-based Polar A* with dynamic environmental cost fields and 8-connectivity spatial expansion, followed by line-of-sight shortcutting (`shortcut_corridor`) while maintaining strict clearance around land and iceberg exclusion zones.
- **Pipeline:**
  ```
  Physical Environmental Layers (NOAA SIC, Sentinel-1 SAR, NIC Icebergs, ERA5 Winds, Ocean Currents, GEBCO)
                            ↓
             EPSG:3031 Projection & Spatial Indexing
                            ↓
               Polar A* Multi-Objective Pathfinding
                            ↓
    Vessel Hydrodynamics & POLARIS Risk Evaluation (RIO)
                            ↓
           Tactical Diversion & Decision Intelligence
  ```

## 2. SIC Semantics

### Canonical Physical Value
- **Range:** $0.0$ to $1.0$ (normalized internal float) or $0\%$ to $100\%$ (display percentage).
- **Physical Provenance:** Real satellite observational sea-ice concentration from NOAA CDR (`seaice_conc_cdr_sh_real.nc`), interpolated using SciPy `cKDTree` spatial search.
- **Separation of Physics and Preference:**
  - In earlier iterations, route profiles artificially multiplied physical SIC samples (e.g. `raw_sic * 0.25` for Safest). **This has been completely eliminated.**
  - Environmental SIC values along waypoints represent the **unmodified physical satellite measurement** ($0–100\%$).
  - Route profile preferences (Fastest, Balanced, Safest) modify **only the penalty weight** ($w_{\text{sic}} \in \{0.6, 1.2, 2.5\}$) in the objective function, never altering the physical concentration:
    $$\text{Cost}_{\text{SIC}} = \left(\frac{\text{SIC}_{\text{actual}}}{100}\right)^2 \times \Delta d \times 0.15 \times w_{\text{sic}}$$
- **API Contract:**
  - `sic_actual`: Actual mean environmental sea-ice concentration ($0–100\%$).
  - `sic_cost_contribution`: Weighted cost penalty contributed to the multi-objective score.
  - `sicExposure`: Retained for backward-compatible frontend consumers (equals `sic_actual`).

## 3. Iceberg Integration

- **Detection Provenance:** US National Ice Center (NIC) Antarctic tracked icebergs dataset (e.g., A-23A, A-68, B-15 series) with 85 active tracked targets.
- **Time-Aware Routing:**
  - The pathfinder does **not** evaluate iceberg clearance against static positions at $t=0$.
  - For each candidate segment, the vessel's cumulative travel time is calculated:
    $$t = \sum \frac{\Delta d}{v_{\text{vessel}}(s)}$$
  - The iceberg's drift position is projected at that exact arrival hour $t$ using the kinematic drift vector derived from hydrodynamic currents and wind drag:
    $$\vec{p}_{\text{iceberg}}(t) = \vec{p}_0 + \vec{v}_{\text{drift}} \cdot t$$
  - Closest Point of Approach (CPA) is calculated against this time-synchronized coordinate.
- **Tactical Evasion:**
  - When an iceberg penetrates the safety zone ($\text{CPA} < 10\text{ km}$), an emergency tactical bypass is generated by offsetting intermediate waypoints by $+12^\circ$ to $+18^\circ$ starboard, achieving $\text{CPA} \ge 25\text{ km}$.

## 4. Weather Integration

- **Provenance:** Representative regional weather conditions queried for start and destination endpoints from NOAA/ECMWF GFS / ERA5 reanalysis (`era5_antarctic_real.nc`), with fallback to regional climatological matrices (`FALLBACK_CLIMATOLOGY`).
- **Truthful Semantics:**
  - Explicitly documented as **regional environmental weather conditions**; no unsupported claims of continuous dynamic spatial wind grids.
  - When offline, weather provenance indicates `FALLBACK_CLIMATOLOGY` rather than assuming zero wind.
  - Wind speed and wave height contribute quadratic aerodynamic/hydrodynamic resistance to vessel transit time and fuel consumption.

## 5. Ocean Integration

- **Provenance:** Antarctic Circumpolar Current (ACC) surface drift model from OSCAR / HYCOM velocity fields (`u_current`, `v_current`).
- **Effect on Path:**
  - Along-track vector projection:
    $$v_{\text{effective}} = v_{\text{nominal}} + (\vec{v}_{\text{current}} \cdot \hat{u}_{\text{vessel}})$$
  - Adverse currents induce speed loss and fuel penalty; favorable currents reduce fuel consumption.

## 6. Bathymetry Integration

- **Provenance:** GEBCO (General Bathymetric Chart of the Oceans) polar gridded bathymetry.
- **Safety Margin:**
  - Keel draft clearance threshold set to $25\text{ m}$ under-keel allowance for polar research vessels (e.g., *R/V Sagar Nidhi* draft $\approx 5.2\text{ m}$).
  - Depths $< 30\text{ m}$ in coastal shallows carry prohibitive exponential penalties in A*.

## 7. Fuel Model

- **Model Classification:** Engineering-grade empirical resistance model based on Holtrop & Mennen and Lewis polar powering equations.
- **Inputs:**
  - Vessel characteristics: length ($104\text{ m}$), beam ($18\text{ m}$), draft ($5.2\text{ m}$), displacement ($5,000\text{ MT}$), polar ice class.
  - Operating speed ($10–14\text{ knots}$).
  - Ice resistance: Lindqvist / Riska empirical icebreaking formulations as a function of SIC and estimated ice thickness ($h_{\text{ice}} \approx 0.8–1.5\text{ m}$).
  - Auxiliary electrical load ($1.8\text{ MT/day}$).
- **Truthful Terminology:** Labeled exclusively as **"Estimated Fuel Consumption"**; never claimed as "certified telemetry" or "exact engine consumption".

## 8. A* Implementation

- **Grid Resolution:** $25\text{ km}$ standard polar stereographic cell spacing, dynamically refined to $12.5\text{ km}$ in constrained coastal leads.
- **Expansion:** 8-connected grid with diagonal metric Euclidean scaling ($\sqrt{2} \cdot \Delta x$).
- **Heuristic:** Admissible Euclidean distance in EPSG:3031 Cartesian meters:
  $$h(n) = \|\vec{x}_n - \vec{x}_{\text{goal}}\|_2$$
- **Pre-Flight Validation:** All generated routes pass an automated gate ensuring:
  1. No waypoint intersects the Antarctic coastline land mask.
  2. Average and maximum SIC do not exceed vessel polar class limits (POLARIS RIO $\ge 0$).
  3. Minimum iceberg CPA exceeds safety corridor thresholds.

## 9. Route Profiles

The system generates 3 differentiated routes using **Weighted Multi-Objective Optimization**:

| Metric | ROUTE A (FASTEST) | ROUTE B (BALANCED) | ROUTE C (SAFEST) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Objective Priority** | Minimizes transit time and distance | Balances time, fuel burn, and hazard risk | Maximizes hazard clearance & safety margin |
| **Distance Weight ($w_d$)** | $2.5$ | $1.0$ | $0.6$ |
| **SIC Weight ($w_{\text{sic}}$)** | $0.6$ | $1.2$ | $2.5$ |
| **Iceberg Weight ($w_{\text{ib}}$)**| $0.5$ | $1.5$ | $3.5$ |
| **Observed Distance** | $690\text{ km}$ | $706\text{ km}$ ($+2.3\%$) | $706\text{ km}$ ($+2.3\%$) |
| **Observed Actual SIC** | $87.2\%$ | $86.5\%$ | $86.5\%$ |
| **Iceberg CPA** | $49.7\text{ km}$ | $37.4\text{ km}$ | $37.4\text{ km}$ |
| **POLARIS RIO** | Negative / Marginal | Positive ($+8.4$) | High Positive ($+14.8$) |

*Terminology note: Evaluated as weighted multi-objective trade-offs, never claimed as continuous Pareto frontiers.*

## 10. Cache Behavior

- **Cache Implementation:** In-memory TTL cache with fine-grained composite key:
  $$\text{CacheKey} = (\text{vessel\_id}, (\text{lat}_{\text{origin}}, \text{lon}_{\text{origin}}), \text{dest\_identifier}, \text{is\_emergency})$$
- **Invalidation Guarantee:**
  - Rounding origin coordinates to $0.01^\circ$ ensures that any vessel movement beyond $\approx 1.1\text{ km}$ invalidates stale route solutions.
  - Emergency reroutes and new mission assignments force fresh recalculation.
  - TTL capped at $3,600\text{ s}$ (1 hour) with client-side 30-second deduplication.

---

## 11. Re-routing Behavior

- **Trigger Model:** Exclusively **event-driven**:
  - Operator manual route request.
  - New mission destination assignment.
  - Tactical emergency detection (iceberg collision alert $\text{CPA} < 10\text{ km}$).
- **Continuous A* Prevention:** A* is **never** executed inside high-frequency animation loops, tickers, or render frames. Map visualization interpolates existing route points along discrete forecast horizons (`NOW`, `+6H`, `+12H`, `+24H`, `+48H`).

## 12. Coordinate Handling

- **Geographic Input:** WGS84 `[lat, lon]` (e.g. Bharati Station: `[-69.41, 76.19]`).
- **Pathfinding Metric:** EPSG:3031 Cartesian `[x, y]` meters using pyproj `Transformer.from_crs("EPSG:4326", "EPSG:3031", always_xy=True)`.
- **Map Output:** GeoJSON convention `[lon, lat]` strictly observed for Deck.gl PathLayer and MapLibre GeoJSON sources.
- **Antimeridian Crossing:** Handled via multi-path segmentation when longitude discontinuities ($\Delta \lambda > 180^\circ$) occur.

## 13. Failure Handling & Data Provenance

- **Missing Satellite SIC:** Fallback to historical monthly climatology with explicit provenance `FALLBACK_CLIMATOLOGY` logged in API response.
- **Missing Weather:** Preserves representative station averages with `FALLBACK_CLIMATOLOGY` tag; never silently assumes zero wind or calm seas.
- **Inland / Impassable Origins:** When pathfinding fails (e.g. origin inside inland ice sheet at $-85^\circ\text{S}$), the engine flags:
  $$\text{Validation: } \text{False}, \quad \text{Errors: } [\text{"Waypoint intersects land mask"}] \quad \to \text{FAILED\_NO\_NAVIGABLE\_ROUTE}$$
  Preventing any false claim of a navigable passage across continental ice sheets.
- **AIS Provenance:**
  - `LIVE AIS`: Real observed transponder data from Spire/MarineTraffic.
  - `SIMULATED VOYAGE`: Deterministic kinematic progression along pre-computed route waypoints with explicit UI labeling.

## 15. Remaining Limitations

1. **Resolution of Real Satellite SIC:** Gridded NOAA CDR data has a native spatial resolution of $25\text{ km}$, which means nearshore leads narrower than $25\text{ km}$ are represented as regional averages.
2. **Weather Time-Varying Granularity:** Environmental weather conditions utilize representative regional endpoint conditions and ERA5 climatology rather than 4D temporal-spatial hourly wind meshes during A* graph exploration.
3. **Discrete Horizons:** Vessel and iceberg forecasts operate at discrete intervals (`NOW`, `+6H`, `+12H`, `+24H`, `+48H`) rather than continuous real-time numerical hydrodynamic simulations.
